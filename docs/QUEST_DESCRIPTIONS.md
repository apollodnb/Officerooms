# Office Rooms – Quest descriptions

All 107 quests as they appear in the quest book (English, like in game).

## I. The Threshold
*How to get in. You should not want to.*

### Memo #001 – *Read me first*
> FROM: Facilities
> TO: New staff
>
> Somewhere in the world there is an office that should not exist.
> Some people find a way in. Most of them do not find a way out.
>
> This book will follow you. It does not judge. It only keeps track.
>
> **How:** Just tick the checkmark.

### The Shrine – *An office in the wilderness* (optional)
> Very rarely, a piece of an office stands alone in the wild: overgrown, lit by candles, with a lectern.
> Its chest holds what you need to open the way.
>
> Hint: it is an Abandoned Office Shrine. With cheats: /locate structure office_rooms:abandoned_office_shrine
>
> **How:** Find an Abandoned Office Shrine in the Overworld (rare, natural biomes, away from villages). Its chest holds a sculk sensor - or mine one in a Deep Dark.

### Something That Falls (optional)
> The rift opens where sand falls onto a sculk sensor.
> Any sand will do. It always does.
>
> **How:** Any sand from a beach or desert.

### A Door Someone Built – *Or: start at a research outpost* (optional)
> There is a second way in. Research teams built outposts under ancient cities, in jungle temples, deep in caves and in the Nether.
> Pull the lever, wait out the warnings, press the red button. The gateway leads into the Office Rooms.
>
> **How:** Find a research outpost (under an Ancient City, beside a Jungle Temple, deep in a cave or in the Nether), pull the power lever, wait for the warnings to end and press the red button.

### The Office Rooms – *However you got here*
> Through a Liminal Rift (sand dropped onto a sculk sensor), through an outpost gateway, or simply because you woke up here.
> You will arrive in a room. The lights will hum. That is normal.
>
> Everything in the Overworld is optional: you never have to go back.
>
> **How:** Enter the Office Rooms any way you like: drop sand onto a placed sculk sensor and walk into the rift, or use an outpost gateway.

## II. First Day at the Office
*Supplies, habits and the first rules*

### Almond Water – *Drink it. Trust it.*
> You will find bottles of Almond Water in desks, cabinets and break rooms.
> Nobody knows who leaves them there. Drink it when you feel wrong.
>
> **How:** Desks, cabinets and break rooms in the offices, hidden rooms, hotel rooms and Level Fun presents.

### Mark the Walls – *Left, right, cross*
> Rooms repeat. You will walk in circles. Chalk on the walls is the oldest trick that still works.
>
> **How:** Office loot (tool drawers, cabinets).

### First Aid – *Paper, paper, wool*
> Craft a bandage. Applying it takes two seconds and you cannot run while you do.
> Plan your two seconds.
>
> **How:** Craft: 2 paper + 1 white wool (shapeless). Also in supply closets.

### A Little Green Light
> Throw a glowstick down a dark corridor before you walk it.
>
> **How:** Office loot, supply closets, hotel rooms, Level Fun presents.

### Industrial Flashlight – *A strong beam, a short life*
> Right-click to switch it on. The charge runs out faster than you think.
>
> **How:** Security offices, maintenance rooms, industrial control rooms and office tool loot.

### Portable Battery – *Steal light from the ceiling*
> Hold the battery in your other hand to charge the flashlight.
> Empty? Drain a lit ceiling light. The room will not thank you.
>
> **How:** Maintenance rooms, industrial control rooms and generator halls, research outposts, repaired fuse boxes.

### Emergency Flare – *Red means back off*
> Most creatures back away from a burning flare. Most.
>
> **How:** Security offices, industrial storage, office tool loot.

### Noise Maker – *Somewhere else, please*
> Throw it and run the other way. Whatever hears it will go and look.
>
> **How:** Security offices, Level Fun presents, office tool loot.

### Level 1 Access Card – *Security doors*
> Some doors in the Office Rooms are locked. Cards are found in special rooms and in the houses of the Neighbourhood.
>
> **How:** Rare office loot, security offices, hotel reception, Neighbourhood houses, industrial control rooms.

### Totem of Mercy – *One way out*
> If you would die in the Office Rooms, it carries you back to the Overworld instead.
> Keep one. Always.
>
> **How:** Very rare: vaults (Maze, Hotel, Poolrooms), secure storage level 3, concealed hidden rooms.

### End of the First Day – *You are still here*
> You have the basics. From here the building opens up in every direction.
>
> Next: Facilities (power and air), Research and the Megastructures.
>
> **How:** Carry 4 Almond Water (nothing is taken).

## III. Facilities
*Power, air and what lives in the dark*

### The Monitors Are Always Right – *Read the screens*
> HAZARD ZONE and POWER warnings appear on the monitors in offices and corridors long before anything happens.
>
> Craft a Hazard Monitor for yourself (or just keep reading the ones on the walls).
>
> **How:** Craft: iron, glass panes and an electronic component.

### Lights Out – *Repair the fuse box*
> Blackouts are announced on the monitors. When the lights die, a fuse box on your floor breaks.
> Find it in the dark and repair it. Repairs are rewarded.
>
> **How:** Wait for a POWER warning on the monitors. When the lights die, find the sparking fuse box on the floor the message names and right-click it holding 3 Electronic Components.

### Electronic Components – *Salvage*
> Server rooms and the industrial complex are the best places to look.
>
> **How:** Server rooms, hidden maintenance rooms and security offices, now and then office drawers; plenty in the Industrial Complex, research outposts and Hazard Zone caches.

### Night Vision Goggles – *See without being seen*
> They run on battery. A portable battery in your hand charges goggles you are wearing.
>
> **How:** Craft: iron, an electronic component, glass panes and leather. Also from research outposts and fuse box rewards.

### Gas Filter – *Charcoal, paper, a nugget*
> A gas mask is worthless without a fresh filter.
>
> **How:** Craft: iron nugget, charcoal and paper (vertical). Also maintenance rooms, supply closets, hotel rooms.

### Gas Mask – *Breathe*
> Hazard Zones poison the air. A mask with a filter keeps you alive in them for ten minutes.
>
> **How:** Office desks and cabinets of almost every room type, hidden rooms, secure storage.

### Special Event Detector – *Where is it this time?*
> Hold it to find an announced or active Hazard Zone.
>
> **How:** Security offices, labs, industrial control rooms, vaults, Hazard Zone caches.

### Toxic Crystal – *Only inside the zone*
> Toxic Crystals grow only inside an active Hazard Zone, guarded by what the gas brings with it.
>
> **How:** Only inside an active Hazard Zone (follow the monitors and the Special Event Detector). Wear a mask with a filter.

### Hazard Visor – *Mask and goggles, fused*
> Combine a gas mask, night vision goggles and a toxic crystal.
>
> **How:** Craft: gas mask + night vision goggles + toxic crystal (shapeless).

### Anomaly Detector – *It never says what it is*
> The closer a creature, the stronger the signal.
>
> **How:** Hidden labs, Neighbourhood houses, research outposts, uncommon office loot.

### Going Up – *Build an elevator*
> Floors are endless in both directions. Elevators make them shorter.
>
> **How:** Craft: iron, a piston, an ender pearl and redstone.

## V. Megastructures

> Tiles and lukewarm water as far as you can see. Somewhere below the surface there is a labyrinth.
>

## VI. Research Outposts (optional)
*Someone tried to build a door*

### Abandoned Outposts – *Lever, warning, button* (optional)
> Optional: everything here is in the Overworld or the Nether.
> In ancient cities, jungle temples, deep caves and the Nether, a research team built small outposts.
> Each one holds a gateway into the Office Rooms. The notes inside explain the procedure.
>
> **How:** Just tick the checkmark.

### Gateway Online – *Pull. Wait. Press.* (optional)
> Pull the power lever, wait out the warnings, then press the red emergency button.
> Stand back.
>
> **How:** Pull the power lever in an outpost, wait out the warnings, press the red button (be within 32 blocks).

### Under the Ancient City (optional)
> The quietest outpost. Sculk all around it.
>
> **How:** Echo shards from Ancient City chests.

### Through the Heat (optional)
> One team went to the Nether. Their outpost is still standing.
>
> **How:** Enter the Nether.

## VII. The Underside
*Beneath the bedrock*

### Underside Key – *Six artifacts, one key*
> Combine the six artifacts of the megastructures.
> Use the key on the bedrock beneath the lowest floor of the Office Rooms.
>
> **How:** Craft from the six megastructure artifacts (shapeless).

### Upside Down – *Fall through*
> Below the bedrock there is only void. Fall into it.
> You land somewhere with grass on the ceiling.
>
> **How:** Use the key on the bedrock under the lowest floor of the Office Rooms and fall into the void.

### [Unknown] – *It burrows*
> Twenty seconds after you arrive, the ground starts to shake.
> Defeat it.
>
> **How:** It appears 20 seconds after you arrive in the Underside. Hit it when it surfaces.

### Noclip – *Walls are a suggestion*
> The Noclip Sword lets you walk through walls (not floors) for ten seconds.
> Two portals appear: one back to the office, one to the Overworld.
>
> **How:** Dropped by [Unknown].

### Above the Top Floor – *Break through the ceiling* (optional)

>
> **How:** Fill a bucket with water.

### Out – *Through the narrow passage* (optional)
> Find the way out of the Manila Room. You will bring something back with you.
>
> **How:** Pour water into the narrow shaft of the Manila Room and swim out.
