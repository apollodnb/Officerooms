# New in Office Rooms 0.44.0 (beta): our own office furniture

**Office Rooms now brings its own office furniture. OfficeCraft is no longer needed or used.**

## Office furniture
- Own designs in the Minecraft style, made of simple shapes, so hundreds of pieces per floor cost little to render
  (also with shader packs):
  - **Office desks** with a drawer pedestal, a computer (a spreadsheet on the screen), keyboard, and now and then a desk
    phone, a stack of papers, a desk lamp or a coffee mug.
  - **Cubicles**: a desk with blue-grey partitions on three sides and an office chair.
  - **Cubicle partitions** in rows, **office chairs** in black, grey and brown.
  - **Conference tables** (5 and 7 long) with papers and mugs, **whiteboards** with a chart that only goes down.
  - **Water coolers**, **printers**, **coffee machines** (vending machines as before).
- Used everywhere the OfficeCraft furniture used to be: the offices, special rooms, outposts, the SS Fun =) and the Dead Mall.
- All pieces are in the creative tab and can be placed and broken like normal blocks.
- `/officerooms furniture showcase` places one of each in front of you.

## Aranea Membri
- Met more often where she lives: cinemas and malls now spawn her more often (`cinemaChance`, `mallChance` 0.12 → 0.3),
  up to two per floor, and closer to you. Configs still at the old defaults are updated automatically.

## Notes
- OfficeCraft furniture that was already placed in a world stays as it is while OfficeCraft is installed. Without
  OfficeCraft those blocks disappear. Furniture that is placed from now on is Office Rooms' own.
- This is also the first step towards a NeoForge 1.21.1 version.
